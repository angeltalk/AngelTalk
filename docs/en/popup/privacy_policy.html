Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2019-01-01

AngelTalk ("we", "us", or "our") operates the AngelTalk mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

The App stores data locally on your device, including photos, videos, and voice recordings that you create within the App. We do not collect or transmit this data to external servers.

2. Camera, Microphone, and Storage

The App requests access to your device's camera, microphone, and storage solely to enable you to create and manage communication cards. This data remains on your device and is not shared with third parties.

3. No Third-Party Analytics

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs that collect personal information.

4. Children's Privacy

AngelTalk is designed to be used by or for children with complex communication needs. We are committed to protecting children's privacy. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
